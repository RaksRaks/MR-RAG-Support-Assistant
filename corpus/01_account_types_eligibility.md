# Account Types & Eligibility — FNB B4 Reference Policy (Synthetic Training Document)

## Overview
FNB B4 offers five core personal account types: Cheque, Savings, Youth, Premier, and Business.
This document sets out eligibility criteria, minimum requirements, and typical use cases for each.

## Cheque Account
- For customers aged 18 and older with a verified South African ID or valid work permit.
- Requires proof of residence not older than 3 months and proof of income for credit-linked products.
- Supports debit order facilities, a linked debit card, and online/app banking.

## Savings Account
- Open to any individual, including minors with a co-signing guardian.
- No minimum balance requirement; interest is tiered based on balance held.
- Limited free withdrawals per month before a transaction fee applies.

## Youth Account
- For customers aged 16–25 with a valid ID or passport.
- No monthly account fee while the holder remains under 26.
- Parental or guardian consent required for account holders under 18.

## Premier Account
- Requires a minimum gross monthly income threshold (set annually by the Premier Banking division).
- Includes a dedicated relationship banker, preferential FX rates, and higher daily transaction limits.

## Business Account
- Requires company registration documents (CIPC), proof of business address, and FICA documents
  for all directors or members with more than 25% shareholding.
- Sole proprietors may use a personal ID in place of company registration documents.

## General Notes
- All new accounts are subject to FICA verification before the account is fully activated (see
  the FICA / KYC Requirements document for detail).
- Account upgrades and downgrades can be requested through the app, online banking, or in-branch.
