# Fees & Charges Schedule — FNB B4 Reference Policy (Synthetic Training Document)

## Monthly Account Fees
- Cheque Account: R89 per month (bundled with 3 free cash withdrawals at FNB ATMs).
- Savings Account: No monthly fee.
- Youth Account: No monthly fee for holders under 26.
- Premier Account: R199 per month, waived if the qualifying income threshold is met.
- Business Account: Tiered from R199 to R899 per month depending on transaction volume.

## Transaction Fees
- Own-bank ATM withdrawal: first 3 free per month, thereafter R8.50 per withdrawal.
- Other-bank ATM withdrawal: R14.00 flat fee plus the other bank's surcharge.
- EFT payment (app or online banking): R3.50 per payment; immediate payments R12.00.
- International SWIFT payment: R450 flat fee plus applicable FX spread.
- Declined debit order due to insufficient funds: R75.00 per instance.

## Card Fees
- Replacement debit card (lost or damaged): R95.00.
- Cross-border card purchase: 2.75% of transaction value in addition to the FX rate applied.

## Waivers
- Fees on the Cheque and Premier accounts may be waived for customers who maintain the required
  minimum monthly credit turnover, as communicated at account opening.
- Students with a valid proof of registration qualify for a 50% discount on the Cheque Account
  monthly fee.

## Disputing a Fee
- Fee queries must be logged through the app's "Dispute a Transaction" flow or via the Complaints
  process (see Complaints & Escalation Process document). Fee reversals are assessed on a
  case-by-case basis and are not automatic.
