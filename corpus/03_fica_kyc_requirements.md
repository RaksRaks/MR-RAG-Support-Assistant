# FICA / KYC Requirements — FNB B4 Reference Policy (Synthetic Training Document)

## Purpose
The Financial Intelligence Centre Act (FICA) requires FNB B4 to verify the identity and
residential address of every customer before an account can be fully activated. This is
Know-Your-Customer (KYC) verification.

## Required Documents (Individuals)
- A valid green-barcoded South African ID, Smart ID card, or passport (for foreign nationals).
- Proof of residential address dated within the last 3 months: a utility bill, bank statement
  from another institution, or a signed and stamped affidavit if the customer resides with
  someone else.
- Proof of income where the account is linked to a credit product (payslip or 3 months of
  bank statements for self-employed applicants).

## Required Documents (Business Entities)
- CIPC company registration documents (or equivalent for close corporations, trusts, and NPOs).
- Certified ID copies for all directors, trustees, or members holding more than 25% ownership.
- Proof of business address and a resolution authorising the account signatories.

## Verification Timelines
- Standard FICA verification is completed within 2 business days of document submission.
- Accounts not fully FICA-verified within 30 days of opening are restricted to deposit-only
  functionality until documents are received.

## Re-verification (FICA Refresh)
- Customers are required to refresh their FICA documents every 5 years, or sooner if flagged
  by an automated risk review.
- Failure to refresh FICA documents on request may result in account restrictions.

## Data Handling
- FICA documents are stored securely and processed in line with the Data Privacy & POPIA
  Compliance document. They are never shared with third parties without customer consent,
  except where required by law or regulator request.
