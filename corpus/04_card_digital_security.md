# Card & Digital Banking Security — FNB B4 Reference Policy (Synthetic Training Document)

## Card Security
- Debit and credit cards use chip-and-PIN and contactless (tap-to-pay) technology with a
  R500 single-tap limit before a PIN is required.
- Customers can instantly freeze or unfreeze a card through the app if it is misplaced.
- A lost or stolen card must be reported immediately via the app, USSD, or the 24-hour call
  centre; the customer is not liable for fraudulent transactions made after the report time,
  provided reasonable care was taken to protect the card and PIN.

## Digital Banking Login Security
- App and online banking access requires multi-factor authentication (MFA): a password or
  biometric login plus a one-time PIN (OTP) sent via SMS or push notification for new-device
  logins and high-risk transactions.
- Sessions time out after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- Customers are advised never to share an OTP with anyone, including staff claiming to be
  from FNB — FNB will never ask a customer to read out an OTP over the phone.

## Suspicious Activity Monitoring
- Transactions are screened in real time against fraud-detection models. Unusual patterns
  (e.g. a large transaction from an unfamiliar location) may trigger a temporary hold and an
  SMS/app verification request before the transaction completes.

## Reporting Compromised Accounts
- If a customer suspects their account or device has been compromised, they should immediately:
  1. Freeze their card(s) via the app.
  2. Change their online banking password.
  3. Contact the fraud line to flag the account for enhanced monitoring.
- See the Fraud & Dispute Resolution Policy document for what happens after a report is logged.
