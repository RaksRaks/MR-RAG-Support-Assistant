# Fraud & Dispute Resolution Policy — FNB B4 Reference Policy (Synthetic Training Document)

## Reporting a Fraudulent Transaction
- Customers must report suspected fraud within 30 days of the transaction date to qualify for
  a full investigation under this policy.
- Reports can be logged via the app's "Report a Problem" flow, the fraud call centre, or in
  branch.

## Investigation Process
- Step 1 — Acknowledgement: the customer receives a case reference number within 24 hours.
- Step 2 — Provisional credit: for card-not-present fraud (e.g. online purchases), a provisional
  credit may be issued within 5 business days while the investigation continues.
- Step 3 — Investigation: the fraud team reviews transaction metadata, device fingerprints, and
  merchant records. This typically takes 10–15 business days.
- Step 4 — Outcome: the customer is notified in writing. If fraud is confirmed, the provisional
  credit is made permanent. If the transaction is found to be authorised or the customer was
  grossly negligent (e.g. sharing an OTP), the provisional credit may be reversed.

## Dispute a Legitimate Merchant Transaction
- Separate from fraud, customers can dispute a charge from a known merchant (e.g. wrong amount,
  goods not received) through the same "Report a Problem" flow. These follow card-scheme
  chargeback rules and can take up to 45 days to resolve, depending on the merchant's response.

## Customer Liability
- Customers are not liable for fraud that occurs after a card or account has been correctly
  reported as lost, stolen, or compromised.
- Customers may be held partially or fully liable if found to have acted with gross negligence,
  such as sharing a PIN, password, or OTP with a third party.

## Escalation
- If a customer is unsatisfied with a fraud outcome, they may escalate via the Complaints &
  Escalation Process document, and ultimately to the Ombudsman for Banking Services.
