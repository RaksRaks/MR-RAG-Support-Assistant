# Loan & Overdraft Facilities — FNB B4 Reference Policy (Synthetic Training Document)

## Overdraft Facility
- Available on Cheque and Premier accounts, subject to an affordability assessment based on
  3 months of account turnover.
- Limits typically range from R1,000 to R150,000, reviewed annually or on request.
- Interest accrues daily on the utilised overdraft balance only, at the customer's individually
  quoted rate linked to prime.

## Personal Loans
- Available to customers who have held an active FNB B4 account for at least 3 months.
- Loan terms range from 6 to 84 months.
- A once-off initiation fee (regulated under the National Credit Act) and a monthly service
  fee apply in addition to interest.
- Early settlement is permitted at any time; early settlement interest is capped in line with
  National Credit Act regulations.

## Application Requirements
- Valid FICA documents (see FICA / KYC Requirements document).
- 3 months of payslips or bank statements reflecting income.
- Existing debit order and account conduct history is factored into the credit decision.

## Missed Payments
- A missed instalment is flagged after 1 day and reported to credit bureaus after 20 days in
  arrears, in line with regulatory reporting timelines.
- Customers experiencing financial difficulty are encouraged to contact FNB B4 before a payment
  is missed to discuss a payment arrangement or restructuring.

## Responsible Lending
- All credit applications are subject to affordability assessment under the National Credit
  Act. FNB B4 will decline or reduce a credit application where the assessment indicates the
  customer cannot comfortably service the debt.
