# Foreign Exchange & International Payments — FNB B4 Reference Policy (Synthetic Training Document)

## Sending Money Abroad
- International payments can be initiated via online banking or in-branch for amounts above
  R500,000 per transaction.
- Each customer has an annual Single Discretionary Allowance (SDA) of R1 million for
  international transfers without South African Reserve Bank (SARB) approval, in line with
  exchange control regulations.
- Amounts above the SDA require a Foreign Investment Allowance application and SARB tax
  clearance.

## Receiving Money from Abroad
- Incoming SWIFT payments are typically reflected within 1–3 business days, depending on the
  sending bank and intermediary banks in the payment chain.
- Recipients may be asked to confirm the source and purpose of funds for regulatory reporting,
  particularly for amounts above R50,000.

## FX Rates
- FNB B4 applies a spread over the interbank exchange rate; Premier and Business account
  holders receive a preferential (reduced) spread.
- Rates are updated continuously during market hours and locked in at the moment a payment is
  confirmed.

## Travel & Card Use Abroad
- Debit cards can be used internationally by default for Premier accounts; other account types
  must enable international usage via the app before travelling.
- A cross-border transaction fee and FX spread apply to all foreign-currency card purchases
  (see Fees & Charges Schedule document).

## Common Rejections
- International payments are most commonly delayed or rejected due to incomplete beneficiary
  bank details (SWIFT/BIC code, IBAN where applicable) or missing purpose-of-payment codes.
