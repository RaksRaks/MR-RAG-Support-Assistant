# Complaints & Escalation Process — FNB B4 Reference Policy (Synthetic Training Document)

## Step 1 — Log the Complaint
- Complaints can be logged via the app ("Help & Support" > "Log a Complaint"), the call
  centre, in branch, or via email to the B4 client service mailbox.
- Every complaint receives a reference number and an initial response within 24 hours.

## Step 2 — Resolution at First Point of Contact
- FNB B4 aims to resolve most complaints within 5 business days. The customer is updated on
  progress at least every 3 business days if the case remains open.

## Step 3 — Escalation Within FNB B4
- If unresolved, or if the customer is unhappy with the outcome, the case can be escalated to
  a team leader, and if necessary, to the B4 Client Experience Office. Escalated cases receive
  a written final response letter within 20 business days of the original complaint.

## Step 4 — External Escalation
- If a customer remains unsatisfied after receiving a final response letter (or if 8 weeks
  have passed without resolution), they may refer the matter to:
  - The Ombudsman for Banking Services (for general banking complaints), or
  - The National Financial Ombud Scheme, or
  - The National Credit Regulator (for credit-related complaints).
- External escalation is free of charge to the customer.

## Complaint Categories Tracked
- Fees and charges, card and digital security, fraud and disputes, loan and credit decisions,
  service quality, and data privacy. Category data is used to identify recurring issues and
  improve the B4 product and service experience.
