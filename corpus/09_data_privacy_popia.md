# Data Privacy & POPIA Compliance — FNB B4 Reference Policy (Synthetic Training Document)

## Legal Basis
FNB B4 processes personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal
Information Act (POPIA). Personal information is collected only for specified, lawful
purposes such as account opening, FICA verification, credit assessment, and fraud prevention.

## What Data Is Collected
- Identity and contact details, financial and transaction data, device and login metadata for
  security purposes, and communication records with the contact centre.

## Customer Rights
- Customers may request access to the personal information FNB B4 holds about them.
- Customers may request correction of inaccurate information.
- Customers may object to processing for direct marketing purposes at any time, without
  affecting their ability to use core banking services.
- Requests are handled by the Information Officer and must be responded to within 30 days.

## Data Sharing
- Personal information is shared with credit bureaus (as required by the National Credit Act),
  regulators (as required by law), and service providers under strict data-processing
  agreements. It is never sold to third parties for marketing purposes.

## Data Retention
- Account and transaction records are retained for 5 years after account closure, in line with
  FICA and tax record-keeping obligations, after which they are securely deleted or archived.

## AI & Automated Processing
- Where AI or automated tools are used (for example, in fraud detection or chatbot support),
  customers have the right to request human review of any decision that has a significant
  legal or similarly significant effect on them.
