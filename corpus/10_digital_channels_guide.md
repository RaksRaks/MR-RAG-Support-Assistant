# Digital Banking Channels Guide — FNB B4 Reference Policy (Synthetic Training Document)

## FNB B4 App
- Full-featured: payments, transfers, card management, statements, loan applications, and
  chat-based support.
- Requires a smartphone with biometric or PIN-based device lock enabled for security.
- Available on iOS and Android; feature parity is maintained across both platforms.

## Online Banking (Web)
- Mirrors most app functionality and is recommended for higher-value transactions and
  document-heavy tasks such as loan applications requiring document upload.
- Requires MFA login (password plus OTP) as described in the Card & Digital Banking Security
  document.

## USSD Banking (*120#)
- Designed for feature-phone access without data or a smartphone.
- Supports balance enquiries, airtime purchase, and basic EFT payments to pre-registered
  beneficiaries only, for security reasons.
- Session charged at standard USSD network rates; no FNB B4 service fee applies.

## Telephone & Chat Support
- 24-hour call centre for card blocking, fraud reporting, and general queries.
- In-app chat support (including an AI assistant for common questions) is available for
  balance, fee, and product queries; complex or account-specific requests are handed off to a
  human consultant.

## Choosing a Channel
- For everyday banking: the App.
- For document-heavy applications: Online Banking.
- For basic banking without a smartphone or data: USSD.
- For lost cards, fraud, or urgent issues: Telephone support (available 24 hours).
